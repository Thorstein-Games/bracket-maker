Make a bracket for anything

Bracket Maker is a free tournament bracket generator for settling the big debates: best pizza topping, greatest movie villain, ultimate fighter. Build it, run it, and crown a champion.

  1. Add contenders. Type names, search for pictures, upload your own images or paste them in. Give each one a description if you like.
  2. Seed the bracket. Pick a size from 2 to 64, then drag contenders into place or hit Auto-fill. Empty slots become byes.
  3. Run it. Each matchup plays out on a fighting-game style versus screen. Pick the winner, or let fate decide.
  4. Share it. Send a link with or without your picks so friends can fill out their own.

FAQ

Is Bracket Maker free?
Yes. It's free, needs no account, and runs entirely in your browser.
How many contenders can a bracket have?
Anywhere from 2 to 64. Pick a bracket size of 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 or 64; empty first-round slots become byes, so odd numbers like 6 or 12 work too.
How do I share my bracket?
Click Share to get a link. The whole bracket is packed into the link, with or without your picks, so friends get their own copy to play.
Where is my bracket saved?
In your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and your bracket is still there when you come back on the same device.
